# v2 roadmap

v1 is deliberately static: synthetic data, deterministic, front end only. v2 is about making it run on real accounts without losing that honesty. Items are ordered by what would most improve meetings per hour.

## Now (first month on real data)

| # | Item | Why | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Move the board into the CRM** | Accounts, score fields and stages should live where the team works | Score and reason fields on account records; the board is a saved view |
| 2 | **Signal capture checklist** | Make Monday's research repeatable for any SDR | A written source list (job boards, company team pages, funding news, partner announcements) with a 2-minute check per account |
| 3 | **Reply tagging** | The Friday review needs structured objections | Every reply tagged interested / not now / wrong person / objection type |
| 4 | **Score validation report** | Prove the score predicts meetings or fix it | Weekly tier-by-tier contact-to-meeting table |

## Next (months 2–3)

| # | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Signal freshness alerts** | A new job ad abroad at a tier-B account should bump it into this week's list automatically |
| 6 | **Sequence A/B on one variable at a time** | Subject line first, then the touch-2 offer, so results can actually be read |
| 7 | **Segment-specific assets** | The touch-2 promises (the country hiring checklist, the contractor self-check, the market-entry sketch) built as real one-pagers, reviewed by Deel's compliance and legal teams |
| 8 | **AE handoff template in the CRM** | The brief on the rhythm page becomes a required field set before a meeting can be marked booked |
| 9 | **Call-block integration** | Tier-A accounts that open or reply are queued for the next call block |

## Later

| # | Item | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Fourth segment: compliance events** | For example, contractor-heavy companies affected by changes to local contractor or employment rules |
| 11 | **Partner-sourced triggers** | Accountants, recruiters and VCs who see expansion plans before they're public |
| 12 | **Territory heatmap** | Where in the UK and Ireland triggers cluster, by city and sector, to focus events and field days |
| 13 | **Team mode** | Shared board across SDRs, with account ownership and no double-touching |

## Principles that carry over from v1

- No signal, no sequence.
- Every number on a screen says whether it's real or sample.
- The score stays explainable: five parts, written down, argued about on Fridays.
- Emails ask before they pitch.
