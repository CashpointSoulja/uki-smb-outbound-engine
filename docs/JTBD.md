# Jobs to be done

## The prospect's jobs (why a UK/IE SMB would take the meeting)

### Hiring abroad
> **When** we've found the right person for a role, and they live in a country where we have no company,
> **I want to** employ them properly and quickly, without setting up an entity first,
> **so I can** get the offer out before they accept somewhere else, and not worry that we've got the contract or payroll wrong.

- **Functional:** compliant contract, local payroll, mandatory benefits, onboarding by the start date.
- **Emotional:** not looking amateur in front of a strong candidate.
- **Social:** the Head of People wants to be seen solving it, not escalating it.
- **Trigger I watch for:** a job ad located in a new country.

### Contractor-heavy
> **When** month-end comes round and we're paying a dozen overseas contractors one transfer at a time,
> **I want to** pay everyone in one run, with proper contracts and invoices on file,
> **so I can** close the books on time and stop worrying that a long-running contractor looks like an employee.

- **Functional:** one payment run, fewer FX fees, invoices and contracts in one place.
- **Emotional:** relief from a monthly chore, and less low-level compliance anxiety.
- **Social:** the finance lead stops being the bottleneck.
- **Trigger I watch for:** team pages, credits or job posts that show a recurring contractor pool abroad.

### International expansion
> **When** we've announced a new market and need people on the ground before the legal set-up is finished,
> **I want to** hire the first few people there now and move them onto our own entity later,
> **so I can** hit the launch date and find out whether the market works before committing to an entity.

- **Functional:** first hires live in weeks, a clear path to our own payroll later.
- **Emotional:** confidence that the launch won't slip because of paperwork.
- **Social:** the COO delivers what the board was promised.
- **Trigger I watch for:** press releases, funding announcements or job ads naming a new country.

## The SDR's job (why this engine exists)

> **When** I start the week with a huge SMB territory and limited hours,
> **I want to** know which 40 accounts have a live reason to talk, why, and what to say,
> **so I can** spend my time on conversations that can become meetings, and hand AEs meetings they're glad to take.

## The AE's job

> **When** a meeting lands on my calendar,
> **I want to** know the trigger, the contact, what they said and what's still unknown,
> **so I can** run a discovery call that starts at minute five, not minute zero.

The AE handoff brief on the Weekly rhythm page is built for exactly this job.
