# ELI5: what is this?

Imagine you run a lemonade stand that sells a special kind of lemonade. It only helps people who are about to do one tricky thing: **pay or hire someone who lives in another country.**

Most people walking past don't need it today. Shouting "LEMONADE!" at everyone is tiring and mostly gets ignored.

So instead you do four things:

1. **Make a list of 40 neighbours who look like they're about to do the tricky thing.** One just put up a sign saying "looking for a helper in Portugal". Another has friends in six countries they send pocket money to every month. A third just announced they're opening a second stand in the Netherlands. *(That's the **prospect board**.)*

2. **Give each neighbour a score.** You ask a few questions. Are they the right size? Did their sign go up recently? How many people abroad are involved? Did they just get some money? Do they lack a stand in that other country? More points means visit them sooner. *(That's the **fit score**, and the **reason** says why in one sentence.)*

3. **Write each neighbour a short, polite note about their own sign.** You don't say "buy my lemonade". You say "I saw your sign about Portugal. Have you worked out how you'll pay your helper?" If they don't answer, you send one helpful tip, then one last friendly note. *(Those are the **three sequences**.)*

4. **Keep a chart of who you've written to, who wrote back, and who said "come round for a chat."** That tells you what's working. *(That's the **pipeline dashboard**.)*

Then you do the same thing **every week**: make the list on Monday, write notes Tuesday to Thursday, and check what worked on Friday. *(That's the **weekly rhythm**.)*

All the neighbours in this version are pretend. They're made up so you can see how it works without bothering anyone real.
